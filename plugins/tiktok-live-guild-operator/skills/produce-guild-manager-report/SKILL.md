# Produce Guild Manager Report

## Assemble evidence

Discover the guild once and read only the domains required by the reporting period. A standard operating report usually needs revenue, creators, recruitment, operations, governance, and selected aggregate intelligence.

Keep each domain's status visible. Use successful domains even if another is partial or unavailable. Every number keeps its unit, period, scope, and observation time. Do not convert unseen records into zero or claim complete coverage from a bounded page.

## Report shape

1. Executive decision: the most important finding and management response.
2. KPI table: current value, comparison, target or threshold, and evidence status.
3. Drivers: proven contributors and explicitly labeled inferences.
4. Priority actions: owner, creator or account ID when relevant, due date, and expected observable result.
5. Risks: compliance deadlines, concentration, resource limits, and data gaps.
6. Next review: what to read again and when.

Prefer a small number of decision-relevant metrics over a raw portal dump. Put detailed IDs or records in a clearly scoped appendix when needed.

## Guardrails

Do not say an action occurred unless live evidence proves it. Do not hide domain errors behind an overall confidence score. The report is an active-task analysis, not a durable source of truth.
