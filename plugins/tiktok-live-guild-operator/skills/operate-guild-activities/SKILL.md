# Operate Guild Activities

## Verify before recommending

Use `guild_operations` to read current activities, Flare Card quota and records, and Promote authorization or coupons. Read creator and revenue evidence separately when deciding who should receive a resource.

For every resource retain its status, eligibility or permission, available quantity, used quantity, validity period, cost, deadline, account scope, and data-delay notice when present. A forecast is not inventory; a coupon is not authorization; missing cost is not free.

## Allocate

Recommend a resource only when all of these are proven:

1. The creator or activity is eligible.
2. Inventory and expiry are visible.
3. The operating objective and measurable baseline are stated.
4. Cost and downside fit the user's limit.
5. A post-activity comparison window is defined.

Use comparable before/after periods and label attribution as uncertain unless a controlled comparison supports it.

## Output

Return available resources, recommended recipients with creator IDs, expected mechanism, required human decision, measurement plan, risks, and unsupported assumptions. Do not claim any resource was assigned or purchased.
