# Plan Three-Month Guild Economics

## Required evidence

Read current and recent-period revenue, incentive tasks, creator activity, recruitment conversion, available activity resources, and material governance constraints. Use `guild_intelligence` for aggregate trends and the responsible domain for current operational details.

If a period, unit, deadline, or population is missing, leave that input unknown. Do not silently substitute zero or extrapolate a complete roster from one page.

## Scenario model

Build three named scenarios:

- Low: observed baseline with conservative retention and activation.
- Base: baseline plus defensible existing-creator, activity, and recruitment increments.
- High: an upper operating case that identifies the extra capacity and risk required.

For each month, show baseline, existing-creator increment, activity increment, qualified new-creator increment, task gap, resource requirement, and confidence. Explain each assumption in business terms.

## Decision rule

Calculate recruitment need only after estimating safe existing supply:

`task gap - natural revenue - existing creator increment - activity increment = recruitment gap`

Treat a negative result as no proven recruitment gap, not as permission to reduce the roster.

## Output

Lead with the recommended scenario, then provide the three-month table, weekly operating actions, trigger points for switching scenarios, risks, unknowns, and human approvals.
