# Operate Guild Revenue

## Read comparable facts

Use `guild_revenue` for overview, incentive tasks, contributions, and business evaluation. Add `guild_intelligence` metrics only when their period and unit match the question. Add creator data only for creator-level attribution.

Retain each metric's raw numeric value, unit, period, comparison period, and observation time. Do not combine diamonds, currency, points, percentages, or estimated incentives without an explicit conversion proven by current evidence.

## Diagnose

Separate:

- total level and recent change
- creator and manager contribution
- concentration among top contributors
- productivity per active creator or LIVE hour when both operands are observed
- official task progress and remaining gap
- business-evaluation constraints

A partial contribution list cannot prove total concentration. A coincident activity and revenue increase does not prove causation.

## Output

Lead with the revenue diagnosis. Provide the metric table, contributor IDs when authorized, concentration scope, task gap, controllable levers, risks, evidence gaps, and the next measurement date. Keep platform facts distinct from forecasts and management recommendations.
